# Clarifications: Patient appointment list, reschedule, cancel and status history

No user was available; the most reasonable option was assumed for each question. Confirm with the client at the next round.

## Q1: How far ahead can a patient reschedule?

- Options: (A) same 30-day horizon as online booking (PD-002); (B) any future date the dentist has free slots; (C) no later than the original date plus 30 days.
- Assumed: **A**.
- Why: the PRD gives one horizon for booking (FR-011, PD-002, ASM-010) and the slot picker is the same component; it keeps the diary manageable. If B is wanted, only the horizon setting changes.

## Q2: Can the patient change the message channel when rescheduling or cancelling?

- Options: (A) no, use the channel chosen for the appointment; (B) show the channel choice again on the change screens.
- Assumed: **A**.
- Why: FR-018 says "in their chosen channel"; FR-013 puts the choice at booking. B would add a screen and the NFR-010 limit of 5 screens.

## Q3: May a patient reschedule to a slot that starts less than 4 hours from now?

- Options: (A) yes, the 4-hour rule applies only to the original start (as the PRD words it); (B) no, the new slot must also be at least 4 hours ahead.
- Assumed: **A**.
- Why: FR-016 states the cut-off only against the appointment being moved, and online booking (FR-011) has no minimum lead time. B would be a new rule the client has not agreed.
