## Summary
The practice manager answers most round 1 questions. Patients choose SMS or WhatsApp for reminders when they book (WhatsApp added alongside SMS). No online payment: patients pay at the clinic. Online cancel or reschedule is allowed until 4 hours before the appointment. Patients can book only 30-minute check-ups, cleanings and consultations online; longer treatments are booked by reception. Two no-shows in 6 months stop a patient booking online until reception lifts it. Dentists sign in to see their own day. Patients sign in with their phone number and a code. English only. No clinical notes in the app. The app must be live on 1 March 2027, when the Whitefield clinic opens its new chairs; a third clinic may open within 18 months. The clinic has no SMS or WhatsApp account yet. She also wants a monthly no-show figure per clinic.

## Points
1. Reminders: "add WhatsApp as a reminder channel alongside SMS; patients choose their channel when booking". (msg 1, para 1)
2. No online payment or deposit; patients pay at the clinic after the visit. (msg 1, para 2)
3. Patients may cancel or reschedule online until 4 hours before the appointment; after that they phone the clinic. (msg 1, para 3)
4. Online booking is for check-ups, cleanings and consultations, 30 minutes each. Root canals, extractions and other longer treatments (60 or 90 minutes) are booked by reception only. (msg 1, para 4)
5. Patients choose a dentist or "first available". (msg 1, para 4)
6. A patient who does not turn up twice in 6 months cannot book online any more; reception can lift that. (msg 1, para 5)
7. Dentists sign in to see their own day; they don't book. (msg 1, para 6)
8. The practice manager sets the dentists' hours and leave for both clinics. (msg 1, para 6)
9. Patients sign in with their phone number and a code; no passwords for patients. (msg 1, para 7)
10. English only. (msg 1, para 8)
11. No clinical notes, X-rays or treatment records in the app; those stay in the clinic's own software. (msg 1, para 9)
12. Go-live on 1 March 2027 is firm: the Whitefield clinic opens 2 new chairs that day. (msg 1, para 10)
13. A third clinic may open within 18 months. (msg 1, para 10)
14. The clinic has no SMS or WhatsApp business account; "you choose, in our name, we pay the message charges". (msg 1, para 11)
15. "I want to see how many no-shows we have each month at each clinic." (msg 1, para 12)
16. Future appointments in the paper diary on go-live day will be typed in by reception. (msg 1, para 13)
