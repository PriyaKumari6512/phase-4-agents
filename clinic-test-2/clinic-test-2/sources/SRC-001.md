## Summary
BrightSmile Dental runs 2 dental clinics in Bengaluru (Indiranagar and Whitefield) with 6 dentists. Every booking is made by phone and written in a paper diary per dentist, so reception is on the phone all day and patients who forget their appointment leave empty chairs: "no-shows are our biggest problem". The clinic wants a simple website where patients book, reschedule and cancel appointments online, receptionists see each dentist's day, check patients in and mark no-shows, and patients get an SMS reminder 24 hours before the visit. It must work on a phone. Insurance claims and mobile apps are not wanted.

## Points
1. BrightSmile Dental has 2 clinics in Bengaluru, Indiranagar and Whitefield, and 6 dentists. (para 1)
2. Today every booking is made by phone and written in a paper diary for each dentist. (para 2)
3. Reception spends most of the day on the phone. (para 2)
4. "No-shows are our biggest problem": patients forget and the chair stays empty. (para 2)
5. Patients book an appointment online. (para 3, bullet 1)
6. Patients reschedule or cancel their appointment online. (para 3, bullet 2)
7. Receptionists see each dentist's day, check patients in when they arrive and mark no-shows. (para 3, bullet 3)
8. Patients get an SMS reminder 24 hours before the visit. (para 3, bullet 4)
9. It has to work on a phone; most patients will book from their phone. (para 4)
10. About 450 appointments a week across both clinics. (para 4)
11. Not wanted: insurance claims, mobile apps. (para 5)
