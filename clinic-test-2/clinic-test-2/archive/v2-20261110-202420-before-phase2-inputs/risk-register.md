# Clinic Booking System — Risk Register

Based on: architecture v2 · Status: proposed

## 1. Scoring
Likelihood and impact are 1–5 (1 rare / negligible, 5 almost certain / severe). Score = likelihood × impact. Rating: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Medium, 10–15 High, 16–25 Critical.

**Id collision (v2).** HLD v2 section 12 reuses RSK-008 (WhatsApp template and opt-in) and RSK-009 (SMS fallback consent) for new risks, but this register already used RSK-008 (hosting cloud) and RSK-009 (SMS provider) under v1, and ids are never reused. Here RSK-008 and RSK-009 keep their v1 meaning. The HLD's new risks are tracked as **RSK-031 (= HLD RSK-008)** and **RSK-032 (= HLD RSK-009)**. Wherever the HLD, ADR-0006, ADR-0007, the LLD flag table or integration-specs write "RSK-008" or "RSK-009" for WhatsApp, read RSK-031 or RSK-032. This is listed as an upstream issue.

Change for v2 (request 2, WhatsApp channel): RSK-031 to RSK-038 are new. RSK-001, RSK-006, RSK-013, RSK-020, RSK-023 and RSK-029 were extended with the new elements (their scores are unchanged). No risk was closed.

HLD risks RSK-001 to RSK-007 keep their ids and meaning. HLD "Low / Medium / High" were read as 2 / 3 / 4 and expanded. RSK-008 onwards are new. The PRD and requests.md contain no open questions and no non-functional requirements, so the open items are the HLD assumptions (ASM-) and the upstream issues from the Data and API step.
