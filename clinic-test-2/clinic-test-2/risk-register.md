# BrightSmile Booking — Risk Register

Based on: architecture v1 · Status: proposed

## 1. Scoring
Likelihood and impact are 1–5 (1 rare / negligible, 5 almost certain / severe). Score = likelihood × impact. Rating: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Medium, 10–15 High, 16–25 Critical.

The HLD rated risks as Low, Medium or High. They are mapped Low = 2, Medium = 3, High = 4, and RSK-001 to RSK-010 keep those scores except RSK-001, which is re-scored (reason in its row). RSK-011 onward are new, continuing the HLD's numbering. No SLA, vendor or price is known for any provider or hosting service, so each is "unknown, to confirm" (RSK-033). Inputs read: HLD, LLD, decisions, data model, integration specs, `scope/scope.md`, `scope/estimate.md`, `product/backlog.md`, `product/prd.md`, `requirements/clarifications.md`, `requirements/register.md`. `requests.md` does not exist in this project folder.
