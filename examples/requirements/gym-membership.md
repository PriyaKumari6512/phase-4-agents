Client: FitZone, a chain of 3 gyms in Pune.

Today members sign up on paper at the front desk and pay monthly in cash or
UPI. Staff track expiry dates in an Excel sheet, so expired members often keep
coming in, and renewals get missed.

We want a simple web app where:
- A new member can sign up online, pick a plan (monthly, quarterly, yearly)
  and pay online.
- A member can see their plan, expiry date and payment history, and renew
  before it expires.
- Front-desk staff can check in a member at the gym by phone number or QR
  code, and the app warns them if the membership has expired.
- The gym owner sees a dashboard: active members per branch, renewals due
  this week, and revenue per month.
- Members get a reminder (SMS or WhatsApp) 7 days and 1 day before expiry.

It must work well on mobile phones. We have about 1,200 members today and
expect 3,000 within a year. Launch target: before 1 January.
